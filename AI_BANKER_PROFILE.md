# AI Banker Profile - Read On Every Run

Who this kit is being run for. Read this immediately after `AI_BASE_RULES.md`, before any search.

## The Banker

- Private banker at J.P. Morgan Private Bank.
- Nominal coverage is $5M+ investable assets.

## How To Use That Number

The $5M coverage figure is context, NOT a filter. Do not screen names in or out against it. Do not build an estimated asset figure and then compare it to a threshold.

Rank by the probability that the person is a real private-bank prospect. A high-probability name with no computable dollar figure beats a name that clears an estimated figure built on a revenue proxy.

The president of a small metals company may sit on generational wealth that no revenue proxy will ever show. Revenue, employee count, PPP job counts, and square footage are weak proxies for household wealth in closely-held businesses - treat them as one signal among several, never as the gate.

## What Raises Probability

Weight these heavily (full standard in the Likelihood Of Fit section of `AI_BASE_RULES.md`):

- multi-generational or closely-held family ownership
- land and mineral holdings
- a recent liquidity event (sale, partial recap, retirement out of an owned business)
- control of multiple entities, locations, or DBAs
- a low public profile - no press, no incumbent private bank calling

## What Still Auto-Rejects

Famous names. A Wikipedia page, Forbes coverage, statewide name recognition, or public-company executive status means the name is already banked. Log it to rejected-duplicates and move on without further searches.

## Contacts

Phone numbers and emails are never hunted with web searches. They come from a single batched ZoomInfo pass after the workbook is built - see Contact Enrichment Pass in `AI_BASE_RULES.md`.
